- Customer knowledge repositories (KAN-475). `ai-dlc knowledge init --customer <name>` writes a
  separate repository that declares `[repo] boundary = "customer"`, with a deny list, CODEOWNERS
  and CI pinned to a release tag. `ai-dlc knowledge promote` moves a sanitised lesson to the team
  repository on a local branch. Check, render and transcript routing refuse anything that would
  leave a customer repository.
- Repositories with customer transcript rules must now list `[transcripts] internal`. Until they
  do, meetings that match no customer, including team meetings with an outside guest, are skipped.
