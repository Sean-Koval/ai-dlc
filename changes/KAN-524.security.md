  `knowledge.toml` declares `[repo] boundary = "customer"` (KAN-524). Elsewhere `get` with the
  record's `project` (CLI and MCP) is refused with the reason, and `search` leaves such records
  out and reports how many under `refused`. `knowledge check` now fails a customer record
  anywhere in a team repository, not only under `[check] roots`, and one of another project in
  a customer repository. A git provider's relative `location` is relative to the project root

  the shell.

  declares (KAN-524 follow-up). A record of another customer that sits in it, under
  `projects/<other>/`, is refused by `get` and left out of `search`, whether or not
  `knowledge check` has run.
