- Parallel PRs no longer conflict on shared files (KAN-481). A PR adds its changelog entry as
  `changes/<KEY>.md` instead of editing `CHANGELOG.md`; `scripts/collect_changes.py` checks the
  fragments and folds them in at release. A PR needn't lower `tests/editorial_baseline.json`
  when a page improves: CI caps each page at its debt on the PR's base instead, and the
  `generated` workflow lists pages to lower after each merge.
