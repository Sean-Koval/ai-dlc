- Customer transcript rules match whole title words, ignoring case, accents and full-width forms, and participants match an
  exact address, a domain and its subdomains, or an exact name; names count only when the provider gives no emails.
  A title with a customer's term only inside a longer word is skipped and logged, not sent to the team repository.
  A one-word `participants` entry is a name, and the pull warns about it: write a domain as `@alpha.example`. A
  domain such as `@io` or `@co.uk` is refused (KAN-490).
